**Bottom line, if you need one sentence per hypothesis (updated 2026-07-18):**
- **H1** (settle/loop/drift regimes) — loop and drift *are* observed in this
  project's own data (B4: 13 loop + 1 drift out of 96 trajectories in
  `forceloop.csv`), but only under an artificially starved compute budget
  (num_steps=16); at full budget only "settle" is seen, and the study is
  underpowered to see loop/drift at the natural rates the literature reports
  there (B8, B9). The compute-budget-forced version (B4, Fisher p=0.01) is
  the project's cleanest positive result and is currently missing from
  README's own Results section.
- **H2** (winding/depth) — **null on the answer token**, two independently
  implemented metrics agreeing (B1, C4) — a real convergent finding in its
  own right, not just a footnote. **Real coefficient, independently
  reproduced, on content tokens** in the unmerged band test (C3) — but as of
  2026-07-18 this is confirmed, not just inferred, to fail the design's own
  stated length-matching bar (its own generating notebook says so, using its
  own printed numbers), and a second replication attempt on a different task
  design (C6) found no dissociation at all. Treat the "content tokens carry
  the signal" framing as an unresolved, promising thread specific to one
  branch/metric combination, not a validated cross-branch synthesis — the
  cross-branch comparison itself has three uncontrolled confounds (different
  `num_steps`, different and differently-sourced loaders, C6 as direct
  counter-evidence). Sensitivity check on the coefficient itself: interaction
  term drops it from +1.30 to +1.09, p<1e-4 to p=0.025.
- **H3** (contraction forces state-holding into loop/drift) — theory is sound
  (A1); empirical signal on real Huginn is small and seed-unstable (B3c) on
  answer-token geometry, and doesn't replicate under the two-scale metric
  (C6). **New (A6, 2026-07-18):** a from-scratch toy-model test (no GPU
  available, so not on real Huginn) found the mechanism does *not* show up
  on a recurrent-over-depth architecture (matching Huginn's own information-
  flow pattern) even under strong contraction, but *does* show up sharply on
  a recurrent-over-time counterpart built to match what the theorem's own
  worked example assumes. If this generalizes, H3's applied claim may need
  to be scoped to architectures where state-tracking depends on surviving
  h_0 — worth the team's own scrutiny, not yet independently reviewed.
  Overall: still not settled either way on the real model; ρ(∂ₕR) has never
  been measured on actual Huginn (A2).
