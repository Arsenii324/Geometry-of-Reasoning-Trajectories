Dear online participants,
This is a reminder that the paper submission deadline is August 9 at 21:00 Moscow time. Papers must be submitted through the OpenReview platform https://openreview.net/group?id=smiles.skoltech.ru/SSPP/2026
The paper review process will then take up to two weeks after submission deadline. Each paper will receive several reviews with  scores and a meta-review with a final decision: Accept or Reject. A project will receive Accepted status if the team submits its final paper and receives an Accept decision following the review process.
Reviewers will assess whether the project is technically sound, sufficiently complete, and clearly presented. They will consider the relevance of the problem, the quality of the methodology and experiments, the significance and reliability of the results, the team’s own contribution, and compliance with the paper requirements. There is no single score that automatically guarantees acceptance. The final decision will be based on the overall reviewer feedback and the quality of the submitted work. Passing the pre-defence and progress-review stages only allowed the team to continue working and does not automatically guarantee final acceptance.
It is particularly important to describe the contribution of each team member in a separate “Contribution” section of the paper.
Several papers may receive an Accept decision (potentially even all submitted papers). We will select the best project among the accepted papers, and its team will be awarded participation in the next SMILES Summer School. This is the main award for online projects.
Please do not be discouraged if your paper is accepted but does not receive the main award. An accepted paper will already have undergone a substantial review process. These reviews may be partially taken into account during submission to the AINL conference, together with additional reviews conducted by the conference. You may also choose to submit your paper to another suitable conference. Additional information about the AINL submission process will be shared closer to January 2027.
